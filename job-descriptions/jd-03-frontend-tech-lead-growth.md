# Target Job Description 03: Frontend Tech Lead / Growth & Product Engineering

**Company:** Kinetix SaaS GmbH *(Simulated DACH / Benelux B2B SaaS)*  
**Location:** Remote (EU)  
**Contract Type:** Full-Time B2B Contractor (`€60 – €75 / hour`)

---

## About the Role
We are seeking a hands-on **Frontend Tech Lead** to lead a newly formed 4–6 person product growth squad focused on **user onboarding, activation, and core product reliability**. You will own technical architecture, set code quality standards, mentor engineers, and partner closely with Product Management to ship high-impact improvements every week.

## Key Responsibilities
- Lead a cross-functional squad of frontend/full-stack engineers alongside a Product Manager.
- Drive measurable product growth metrics (activation rate, onboarding completion, conversion uplift) through rapid experimentation and clean architecture.
- Establish automated testing (**Jest, Playwright, Cypress**), CI/CD pipelines, and observability practices to bring defect rates down and enable frequent, safe releases.
- Design technical hiring assessments and lead technical interviews to grow the engineering team.
- Bridge frontend architecture (**Angular / Vue / React / TypeScript**) with backend APIs (**Node.js**) and product analytics.

## Required Qualifications
- **7+ years** of software engineering experience with at least **1+ year** in a Tech Lead / Team Lead or mentoring role.
- Proven experience leading an engineering team to measurable product KPIs (activation, conversion, bug reduction).
- Experience designing technical test projects and interviewing engineering candidates.
- Strong product intuition, UX/UI collaboration skills (**Figma**), and clear English communication with both engineers and non-technical stakeholders.
