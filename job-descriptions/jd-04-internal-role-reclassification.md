# Internal Role Reclassification Brief 04: Annual Scope & Compensation Review

**Purpose:** Prepare for an internal compensation & scope reclassification conversation when your actual engineering responsibilities have outgrown your original contract.  
**Scenario:** Your original role was scoped as an individual frontend contributor with a standard annual cap (e.g., `+5%`), while over the past 12–18 months you expanded into full-stack ownership, mentoring, and hiring.  
**Target Outcome:** Formal **Role Reclassification** (Senior Full-Stack / Tech Lead scope + market-aligned rate adjustment) and **Workload Rebalancing**.

---

## Gap Between Original Scope vs. Actual Scope Delivered

| Dimension | Original Contract Scope | Actual Delivered Scope (Last 12–18 Months) |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Application Ownership** | Frontend UI tickets within one app | End-to-end ownership of core checkout, onboarding, backoffice, and embedded merchant widgets |
| **Backend & Infrastructure** | None / minimal | Active development on **Node.js / Express** services, payment integrations, and **CDN** optimization |
| **Delivery Velocity** | Standard individual contributor share | Consistently carrying critical-path feature delivery plus architectural stabilization |
| **Invisible / Unpointed Work** | None | Dependency security upgrades, deep architecture research, profiling, and optimization spikes |
| **Business & Revenue Impact** | Indirect feature delivery | Measurable load-time reductions, conversion uplift, and direct revenue protection |
| **Stability & Legacy Debt** | Standard bug fixes | Permanently resolved recurring multi-year edge-case defects across critical user flows |
| **Hiring & Technical Leadership** | Individual contributor | Designed technical assessments and leads technical interviews for incoming engineers |

---

## Non-Monetary Ask (Workload & Sustainability)
1. **Make invisible work visible:** Create tracked Spike / Tech Debt / Maintenance tickets for dependency upgrades, research, and performance investigations.
2. **Rotate routine maintenance:** Distribute routine dependency reviews/upgrades across the engineering team in a weekly rotation so senior bandwidth stays focused on high-impact architecture.
