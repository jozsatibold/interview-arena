# Target Job Description 01: Senior Frontend / Checkout Engineer (EU Fintech / BNPL)

**Company:** PayFlow Europe *(Simulated European BNPL & Checkout Scaleup — comparable to Scalapay / Billie / Pleo)*  
**Location:** Remote (EU Time Zone)  
**Contract Type:** Full-Time B2B Contractor (`€55 – €68 / hour`)

---

## About the Role
We are looking for a **Senior Frontend / Checkout Engineer** to own our high-conversion checkout experience, merchant onboarding flows, and payment integrations across European e-commerce markets. You will work directly with Product Managers, UX Designers, and Backend Engineers to make our checkout faster, more resilient, and more profitable.

## Key Responsibilities
- Own architecture, development, and performance optimization of our customer-facing **Checkout, Signup, and Payment Recovery** web applications.
- Collaborate with backend teams on **Node.js (Express/NestJS)** BFF services and third-party payment provider integrations (**Stripe, Adyen, Bambora**).
- Improve Core Web Vitals, bundle sizes, and time-to-interactive for latency-sensitive payment flows.
- Analyze user funnel behavior and partner with Product to run A/B experiments and UX improvements that boost conversion rates.
- Write and maintain automated tests (**Jest, Playwright, Cypress**) to guarantee zero-regression releases on financial flows.
- Participate in technical hiring, mentor mid-level engineers, and help raise frontend architecture standards across an **Nx** monorepo.

## Required Qualifications
- **6+ years** of professional experience in frontend/full-stack web development.
- Deep production mastery of **TypeScript**, **Angular** (RxJS, NgRx/reactive state management), and modern monorepo tooling (**Nx**).
- Experience working with **Node.js** backend services and integrating payment APIs.
- Proven track record of measurable performance optimization (load time, bundle reduction) and conversion-focused engineering.
- Fluent working proficiency in English (EU time zone availability).

## Nice to Have
- Experience with Web Components (**Lit**), CDN caching strategies, or merchant-embedded scripts.
- Prior experience in **Fintech, BNPL, or E-Commerce Checkout** domains.
- Experience conducting technical interviews or leading small cross-functional teams.
